# Waiter — quick guide

Connect to Wi-Fi **The Chinese Chef** and open the DineSense app. The server is pre-filled as `http://192.168.1.43`.

1. Open the DineSense icon. Enter your **PIN**.
2. Tap **Sound is off — tap to start your shift** once. Keep the phone speaker on.
3. **Next up** shows the one thing to do now. Tap **Acknowledge** when you are
   going, **Snooze 5m** if the customers are waiting for friends, or **Done** when
   finished.
4. **Floor:** tap a table to order. Use **More actions** for bill, moving a table,
   ignoring the camera for 5 minutes or setting a status by hand.
5. **Order:** tap a size (Half/Full, Dry/Gravy), add notes, then **Send to kitchen**.
   The price shown is for that table's floor.
6. When the kitchen marks food ready, the alert says “food is ready — pick up”.
   Serve it, then tap **Mark served**.
7. Customer asks for the bill: **More actions → Request bill**.
8. **+ New parcel** creates a takeaway order.
9. No signal? Orders are queued on the phone and sent once when the Wi-Fi returns.
   Do not re-enter them.
10. **Cameras** shows the live table view; if a camera is offline, set tables by
    hand.
