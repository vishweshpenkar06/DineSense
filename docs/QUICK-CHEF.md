# Chef — quick guide

1. Open the DineSense icon and enter your **PIN**.
2. Tap **Start shift** once for the chime and to keep the screen awake.
3. Tickets arrive in **New**. Oldest is first; the age turns amber at 10 minutes
   and red at 20.
4. Tap **Start** when you begin cooking, **Ready** when it leaves the pass. The
   waiter is alerted immediately.
5. **By dish** groups the same item across tickets (e.g. Hakka Noodles ×3).
6. **Stock:** tap a variant to mark it out of stock; it greys out on waiter phones
   immediately. Tap again to restock.
7. Cancelled tickets flash red with a different tone; do not cook them.
8. Notes (less spicy, no onion-garlic, Jain, …) are shown in amber — read them.
