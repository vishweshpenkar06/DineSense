# Reception — quick guide

1. Sign in with your **PIN**.
2. **Open visits** lists tables and parcels with running totals. Orange = bill
   requested. Red = a waiter has not responded — check the floor.
3. Select a visit to open its bill. The bill number is assigned automatically.
4. Add **extras** (water, cold drinks) with one tap; add a fixed or percent
   discount; void a line item with a reason if needed.
5. Check the GST breakup, service charge and total. For UPI, show the QR on screen or print, then confirm payment in the app. Split across **Cash / UPI / Card**; the change is shown if the customer pays more.
6. **Take payment & close** → the bill is PAID and the table becomes “needs
   cleaning”.
7. **Print** and choose 80 mm thermal or A4. The bill shows **The Chinese Chef**, GSTIN, FSSAI, HSN, GST breakup, bill number, items, payments, UPI QR and footer.
8. **Void bill** removes an unpaid bill (reason required, number kept). Admin can
   **reopen** a paid bill; payments are reversed and recorded in the audit log.
9. If the system health banner is red, tell the owner/check Admin → Health.
